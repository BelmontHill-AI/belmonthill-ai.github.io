Belmont Hill in 3D

Built from public data: real ground, real building shapes and heights, real tree positions. A proof of concept.

Controls
  • Drag to orbit, right-drag or two fingers to pan
  • Scroll or pinch to zoom
  • Click the minimap to fly there
  • WASD walk, mouse looks
  • Shift run, Esc frees the mouse
  • On a phone: left stick walks, drag right side to look
What's real: ground and tree heights from the USGS 3DEP laser scan (2021); roofs and ground from MassGIS aerial photos (spring 2025, 15 cm); building outlines from OpenStreetMap, plus a few traced from the laser scan.
What's drawn: walls, windows, doors and the fall leaves, styled after the school's 360° campus tour; extra building names from the 2022 campus map. Fields are lined to regulation sizes; grass, turf and clay textures are CC0 from ambientCG.
Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
